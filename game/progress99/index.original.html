99%

Support This Game

IT'S JUST A LOADING SCREEN. IT'S AT 99%.

Click to help it finish. It doesn't want your help.

The longer you stay, the more it knows about you.

5 ACTS · 6 FAKE FIXES · 16 POPUPS · 1 ENDING · ∞ DECIMALS

▶ No download. Plays in your browser. Works on mobile.

FEATURES

  • 5 Acts: Slides from "Reticulating splines..." into something that knows what time it is.
  • Infinite Decimals: 99.1, 99.12, 99.123... every click makes it longer.
  • Fake Fixes: Clear cache, Restart router, Download more RAM, Pray. (They actually help.)
  • The End: A real ending worth screenshotting.
  • Endless Mode: Unlocks after the ending. Every digit is a slot spin.
  • Respects Your Time: About 9 minutes for a full run. Saves automatically.

CONTROLS

  • Interact: Click / tap anywhere (or Space).
  • Spend Progress: Click the "Taking too long? Try:" links to spend progress.

Play it with the sound on.

Please stop clicking.
