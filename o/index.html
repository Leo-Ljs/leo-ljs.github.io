ONE OF THESE IS YOU

Support This Game

ONE MOUSE. TWENTY LEVELS. ONLY ONE OF THEM IS YOU.

Move your mouse. They all move.
One of them moves like you. The rest are mirrors, echoes, rotations and liars. Your real cursor is hidden: the only way to find yourself is to feel it.

20 levels · 6 kinds of reflection · 1 hand · 0 words

▶ No download. Plays instantly in your browser.

🪞 FIND YOURSELF

Mirrors, echoes that repeat you half a second late, twins at half and double speed, a shell game, a crowd of twelve almost-yous.

🔑 USE YOURSELF

Send your reflection for the star. Stand on the plate so it can walk through the door. Let your echoes die on the cracked walls. Let your past self hold the door open for you.

👻 BECOME THE CROWD

Every time you die, that attempt stays behind as a see-through cursor replaying exactly what you did. At the end, all of them come back.

🎮 CONTROLS

Move the mouse. That's it. (Touch: drag anywhere.) · M mute · R give up this attempt

Psychologists call it the "sense of agency": you know which body is yours because it does what you meant, a split second after you meant it. This game plays with that.

Sound on. Made by raxeris.
